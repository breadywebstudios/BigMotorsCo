Where this comes from

It started on
the Autobahn.

Big Motors was started by someone who grew up in Germany, on roads with no speed limit, at an age when a 911 going past is an event.

The 964 and the 993 were the cars of that childhood. Ordinary traffic there. Never ordinary to him.

The first Porsche he bought himself was a 997 Turbo. Same shape, same badge, a long way from the roadside. That car is the reason this company exists.

Big Motors is a second business. The first is a consulting practice. This one was built out of the cars, and it is run by people who would own most of what passes through it.

We do not need your car to sell this month. There is no number set by somebody else, no quarter to close, and nothing here we would be embarrassed to stand next to. When we tell you what your car is worth, nothing on our side is pushing the figure.

What representation is worth

Same car. Same market.
Presented right.

The same car can make two very different numbers, and usually nothing about the car explains the gap. Two of the cars we have sold had already been through auction. Both failed there. Neither car was changed afterwards, and neither market moved.

A competition car carrying an FIA passport valid to 2031, which is the detail that decides who may buy it and what it is worth to them. Auction went as far as it was going to go. We sold it a few weeks later, to a buyer who understood the passport.

How a car is represented

We know what
to look for.

Years of buying, selling and inspecting in this market produce a clear picture of what a good example looks like, and what a well presented one looks like. They are not always the same thing.

The inspection

  • Paint thickness on every panel.
  • Service history read for what is missing, not only for what is there.
  • Options checked against what should be present for the production year and the specification.
  • Matching numbers, checked across the car. ChassisEngineGearboxColourTrim

The presentation

  • Photography that shows the car as it is.
  • A written description that explains the car rather than lists it.
  • Private first. It goes to buyers we already know before it goes anywhere public.
  • Every enquiry qualified before anybody stands next to it.
